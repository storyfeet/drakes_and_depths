Goals
======

*The Goals shouldn'd change too much as this will be the focus of the project*

A story system, where my character abilities and traits affect the outcome of the story.

Simpler than Dnd To run and learn.

PC details to include enough information about who they are and what they value to affect how they play.

Be able to play simple, one-shot linear adventures without over engineering the characters.

Be able to play longer camplains without feeling the characters are missing something important.

Able to handle fights, and dungeons that takes into account character traits, strenghs and weaknesses.

A quick way to throw together characters, but also an ability to make more complex characters with more effects.

To be able to handle different kind of weapons, combined with player ability
